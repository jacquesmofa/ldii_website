# Editing the LDII website

Two editors change the same website. Use whichever you prefer. Every save goes live by itself in about 4 minutes.

| | Pages CMS | Sveltia |
|---|---|---|
| Address | https://app.pagescms.org | https://ldiinitiatives.org/admin/ |
| Best for | Team members without a GitHub account (invited by email) | People with a GitHub account |
| Sign in | Email link, after Jacques invites you | "Sign In with GitHub" |

## What is where

1. **News, events, gallery, partners**: the things that change often.
   - *In the news*: articles about LDII with "Read more". The first one is the large card on the home page.
   - *Events*: give each event a date (year-month-day). Future dates show under Upcoming; past dates move to Past Events and the home page by themselves.
   - *Photo gallery*: open an album and add photos, or add a new album. A new album gets its own page automatically.
   - *Partners*: confirmed partners only. A partner with a logo also appears in the logo row on the home page.
2. **Our work**: the six international focus areas, the six local programmes, the newcomer areas, the About text.
3. **Site settings**: name, emails, phone, address, social links, the menu, the footer, the home film, and what Google and WhatsApp show for each page.
4. **Page headings and text**: the wording of each page, section by section.
5. **Legal pages**: privacy, terms, accessibility.

## Rules that keep the site trustworthy

- Real facts, real photos, real logos only. If a number, partner or name is not confirmed, leave it out.
- Photos: JPG or PNG, under 2 MB, landscape when possible. Always fill in the picture description.
- "Web address name" and "Short ID": lowercase words joined by dashes (youth-forum-2027). Never change one after publishing: links would break.
- The mining mission is the "Global South–Canada Mining & Technology Transfer Mission". Do not write "Africa–Canada".
- LDII is a nonprofit, not a registered charity: never promise tax receipts.

## If something goes wrong

- **The change does not appear after 10 minutes.** The website checks every page before it publishes. If your change would leave a page blank, nothing is published and the site stays as it was. Open the entry again, undo or complete the last change (a new item usually needs at least a title), and save.
- **You deleted something by mistake.** Nothing is lost: every save is kept in the history. Tell Jacques the time of the save and it can be restored.
- **You cannot sign in to Sveltia.** Use Pages CMS in the meantime and tell Jacques.
